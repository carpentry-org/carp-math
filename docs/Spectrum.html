Spectrum

db

defn

(Fn [(Ref (Array Complex) a)] (Array Double)) 

                        (db arr)

returns the decibel (dB) log-magnitude spectrum of the complex array: 20 * log10(magnitude). Clamps small values to avoid log(0).

log-spectrum

defn

(Fn [(Ref (Array Complex) a)] (Array Double)) 

                        (log-spectrum arr)

returns the natural log magnitude spectrum of the complex array.
